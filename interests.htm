Deep Dives

Interests & Philosophy

Beyond the technical and the professional—exploring soundscapes, existential mindsets, and the disciplines that shape perspective outside working hours.

Module_01 // Melophile

Sound Archive

Retro is the default setting, across three languages. Golden-era Hindi playback from Kishore Kumar, Mukesh, and Mohammed Rafi; ghazals from Jagjit Singh; Punjabi folk-fusion and qawwali from Satinder Sartaaj and Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan; and classic English from Elvis Presley, Engelbert Humperdinck, B.J. Thomas, and James Elliot Reay.

I tend to listen to full albums rather than single tracks, and the rotation is fairly varied — lo-fi while working, film scores while reading, city pop on a walk. Nothing more dramatic than good taste in playlists. Balanced out with a handful of today's era for the quieter, soothing side — Arijit Singh, Atif Aslam, Uday Benegal, and Prateek Kuhad in heavy rotation.

Module_04 // Philosophy

First Principles

Drawn to stoicism, existentialism, and epistemology — less for the debate, more for the toolkit. Marcus Aurelius and Camus share a shelf for a reason: both are ultimately about how to act well under uncertainty. Drawn to thinkers who turn ideas into a usable toolkit rather than a debate: Osho and Swami Vivekananda on the mind, Aristotle on reasoning from first principles, and Rabindranath Tagore for proving philosophy and art don't have to live in separate books.
